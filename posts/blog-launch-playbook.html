Blog Launch Playbook: From Zero to Live 🚀

The exact structure I used to avoid chaos and ship fast without breaking quality.

2026-06-01 4 min read Launch

1. Sequence beats intensity

I started with structure, then styling, then deployment. That order keeps momentum clean and avoids random rewrites.

2. Ship small first

The first version was intentionally minimal. Once live, I iterated fast in public and improved the experience step by step.

3. Stabilize after launch

After going live, I refactored content into centralized files. That made every future update faster and less fragile.
