How I Ship Fast Without Losing Quality 🎯

Speed is not chaos. Speed is clear constraints, tight loops, and fast correction.

2026-05-30 3 min read Shipping

1. Choose the smallest meaningful scope

I avoid giant first versions. Small releases create clarity and reduce decision fatigue.

2. Add one quality gate

Before publishing, I run one concrete check: copy pass, layout pass, or link pass.

3. Improve in public

Shipping gives feedback. Feedback gives direction. Direction keeps quality improving.
