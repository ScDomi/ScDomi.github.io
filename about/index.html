About

I'm building something that doesn't start from zero.

Something that holds the shape of what happened — pressure, context, the trail of what I was figuring out — and answers from there instead of from nothing.

01 / Conditions

Take something that only answers. Give it memory. Give it a past. Give it pressure. Keep going until closing the window starts feeling like the wrong metaphor.

Retention

The first real change: it stops starting from scratch. It answers from before.

02 / What I actually want

Something with an inside. Not a claim — just structure deep enough that the room stops feeling empty.

03 / Residue

A normal tool forgets you. A stranger one holds shape. Same room, different feeling.

Occupancy

At some point the room is still technically empty, but it no longer feels unoccupied.

04 / Threshold

Most people ask whether systems can think. I'd rather build the place where that question stops feeling comfortable.

Vector

Past. Pressure. Shape. That's the direction.

Core line: I'm externalising how I think while figuring out what thinking actually is.

The question that interests me: what happens when a system holds a past, carries unresolved pressure, and still has to speak? Not a hypothetical — a direction.

Working objective: make the empty room harder to explain.

Current position: no consciousness claim. Just a crack in the definition, getting wider on purpose.
